# Week 06

So far in this course, my understanding of creative coding has changed a lot. At the beginning, I saw coding mainly as something technical and quite separate from drawing. Now I see it more as a creative system — a way to build rules and processes that generate visual results instead of directly designing a single image.

I noticed a strong difference between analog and digital work. Drawing by hand feels immediate and intuitive, while coding requires translating ideas into logic and parameters. That felt limiting at first, but over time I started to enjoy how simple rules and randomness can create complex and unexpected outcomes.

Through projects like the pattern exercise, the expressive clock, and the drawing machine, I learned that systems can be poetic and emotional, not just functional. I became more comfortable experimenting, letting go of full control, and treating the machine as a collaborator rather than just a tool.

I was sick sometimes during the course and missed a few things, but I used that time to catch up and reflect on the material. That actually helped me connect the different topics better and understand how patterns, time, and machines are all about processes and transformation.

Overall, I feel more confident working with creative code and more open to exploration, uncertainty, and surprise.
