# Week 13

The second version of the project already felt much better than the first one, but feedback helped me see that the essence of the project wasn’t always clear to others.


Some people questioned what the project was really saying about time. That surprised me, because the concept felt obvious to me — but not yet to others. This pushed me to think more about communicating meaning, not just visuals.
The connection between the visual sand effect and the idea of time value wasn’t always explained enough in the text.
I want to make the concept readable without me being there to explain it — clearer wording, clearer intent, and maybe clearer UI.
I want to refine how the interaction and visuals relate directly to the idea of time, not just as an effect but as part of the meaning.
